## Description

Acoustic guitar cover of Simple Man by Lynyrd Skynyrd. Music is a solitary refuge for me so it took a lot for me to post Comfortably Numb last year (https://youtu.be/Khf-N2f8T78) and even more to sing on this one. Let me know if you have song requests for next year, and maybe before. Maybe one day I'll play an original song as well.

This song was recorded with Ableton Live. It has 3 guitar tracks. The video shows me playing one of the tracks. Next time, instead of layering, I'll just do it with a single track and a loop pedal like I would if I was playing it live. I'm new to recording music. The goal is to always be improving and enjoying the process, in all things, which I do.
