## Description

When self-driving fleets become the dominant way of getting from point A to point B, what will the "rules of the road" be? How could autonomy and self-driving cars affect organizations and government regulatory processes like the DMV? How could this all affect the justice system -- in both big and small ways, from crimes to resources spent on traffic violations? 

Frank Chen (head of research, deal, and investing at Andreessen Horowitz), considers these questions in this set of videos -- which can be viewed together all at once or separately -- that goes beyond just self-driving cars to the overall autonomy ecosystem...
