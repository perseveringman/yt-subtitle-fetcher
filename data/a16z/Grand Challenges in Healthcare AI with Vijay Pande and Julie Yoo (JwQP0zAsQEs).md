## Description

In this episode, Julie and Vijay discuss the transformative potential of AI in the healthcare industry, an area traditionally resistant to technological adoption.

08:08 - Enhancing healthcare scheduling and reducing waste
10:16 - The role of AI in optimizing healthcare systems
