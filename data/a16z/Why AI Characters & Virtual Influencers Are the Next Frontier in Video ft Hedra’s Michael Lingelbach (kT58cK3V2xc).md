## Description

In this episode, a16z partners, Justine Moore and Matt Bornstein are joined by Michael Lingelbach, founder and CEO of Hedra, to unpack how generative video is evolving, and why the future of AI characters may look more like interactive co-stars than static clips.

They cover:

- How Hedra went from viral memes and talking babies to powering enterprise video at scale
- The rise of virtual influencers and autonomous digital personas
- Why Hedra’s “character-first” approach is different from traditional video models
- The technical and UX challenges of making AI feel truly alive — from voice cloning to interactive emotions
- How real-time generative video could reshape customer support, education, and news
- The founder’s journey: building at breakneck speed, staying close to users, and leading by example
- Where the next wave of AI-native content creation is headed — and why prompt-to-video may soon feel outdated

Along the way, they discuss creative edge cases (talking trash cans, penguin avatars), the tension between control and automation in multi-modal models, and what it takes to build a team that ships AI products at high velocity.
