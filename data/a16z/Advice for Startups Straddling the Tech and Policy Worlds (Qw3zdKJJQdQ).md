## Description

The political world has “woken up” to the fact that tech is more than a minor business sector and is "transforming life in a big way". But it also wants to make sure that people reap the advantages not risks of new tech. So what is the one piece of advice Ed Felten -- who bridges the tech and policy worlds -- would give to startups trying to do something new in the tech space? Felten is both a professor in both computer science and public affairs at Princeton University (as well as Director of its Center for Information Technology Policy) -- and also served as the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's first Chief Technologist from 2011-2012. This video was recorded at the Andreessen Horowitz Academic Roundtable that took place in September 2014.
