## Description

As we transition to a world of self-driving cars, where will the money for new public infrastructure come from, how will we spend it... and 
could it affect decision-making about cities, suburbs, and other public spaces?

And how and when do we think this will actually happen? Frank Chen (head of research, deal, and investing at Andreessen Horowitz), considers these questions in this set of videos -- which can be viewed together all at once or separately -- that goes beyond just self-driving cars to the overall autonomy ecosystem...
