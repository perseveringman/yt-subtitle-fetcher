## Description

In this talk, Chris Power, the founder and CEO of Hadrian, will walk us through how his company is modernizing aerospace and defense manufacturing. He will explain why our current state of affairs — which includes reliance on thousands of small machine shops — is not sustainable, and how software-based processes can result in faster, less expensive manufacturing. Power will also discuss Hadrian’s process for rapidly training employees, and how a skilled workforce will improve America’s capabilities across a variety of high-tech sectors that are critical to its future.
