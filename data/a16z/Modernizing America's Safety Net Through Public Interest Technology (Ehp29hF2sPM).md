## Description

Traditionalists might argue that the fight against poverty is solely the domain of the government, but, in reality, lots of private companies have played a significant role in improving education, healthcare, and financial services. In this presentation, Propel Founder and CEO Jimmy Chen will walk through how technology and the government can work together to help low-income Americans improve their financial health, and what future opportunities he sees in the field.
