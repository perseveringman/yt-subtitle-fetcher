## Description

How much money should you raise for your startup? Should you fundraise out of the gate or try to bootstrap the company to start? How do you think about your investors as part of your company strategy, rather than just as a source of cash?

a16z General Partner Andrew Chen (@andrewchen) and serial entrepreneur Justin Kan (@justinkan) dive into these questions. Justin has founded several companies, including Twitch (sold to Amazon) and now Atrium, a software-powered law firm.

This clip is part of a16z Entrepreneurship Advice, a series of mini-lessons on how to start, build, and scale startups.
