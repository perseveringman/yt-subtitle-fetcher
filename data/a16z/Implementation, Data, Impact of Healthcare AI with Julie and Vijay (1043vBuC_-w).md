## Description

In this episode, Julie Yoo and Vijay Pande dive into the potential and challenges of implementing AI in healthcare. They revisit the grand challenges discussed in Part 1 of this conversation, Grand Challenges in Healthcare AI, and address new questions that have emerged, focusing on three main areas: the cost of healthcare and AI implementation, the data landscape for AI training, and patient experience.

Topics covered: 
00:00 - Intro
00:03 - Overview of three clusters of Part 1 questions: cost, data, and patient experience
00:30 - Vijay Pande discusses AI's potential to lower healthcare costs
03:00 - Julie Yoo examines the impact of AI on cost structure and labor
06:39 - Challenges in reducing healthcare costs in America
08:15 - The role of AI in shifting from sick care to preventative care
12:00 - Financial incentives and barriers to AI adoption among clinicians
15:39 - Emotional and practical aspects driving AI adoption in healthcare
19:42 - The importance of data sharing and the role of AI in healthcare innovation
22:36 - The data advantage (or lack thereof) of legacy healthcare companies
24:00 - Balancing AI-driven recommendations with clinician expertise
27:00 - The role of AI in enhancing patient experience through choice and automation
30:00 - Long-term vision for AI in personalized and preventative healthcare
