## Description

What happens when AI meets the most personal parts of our lives—our minds and our bodies?

In this episode, a16z's Bryan Kim is joined by Jonathan Swerdlin (Function Health) and Daniel Cahn (Slingshot AI / Ash) to explore how founders are reimagining healthcare and mental health from the ground up using AI. They discuss:

- Why the most impactful AI experiences start with real human context—not just digital behavior
- Building trust in consumer health products outside of traditional systems
- Creating AI that supports—not replaces—doctors and therapists
- What “hourly active use” looks like when AI truly helps

From biomarker-driven health insights to AI-powered therapy, this conversation dives into how founders are reshaping care for a more accessible, continuous, and deeply personal future.
