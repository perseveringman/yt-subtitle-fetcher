## Description

When many companies go remote, they try to copy and paste the office environment into the virtual environment. On the a16z Podcast, GitLab’s Head of Remote Darren Murph explains why this doesn't work, and that fully remote companies can create a more productive culture by prioritizing speed of knowledge retrieval over speed of knowledge transfer.
