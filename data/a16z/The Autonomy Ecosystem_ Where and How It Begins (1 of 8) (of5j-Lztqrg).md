## Description

Discussions of autonomy and self-driving cars focus so much on the tangible artifact -- the car itself -- but so much more will change *beyond* the car... from public infrastructure, the automotive value chain, and energy, to finance (including insurance), the justice system, and yes, shopping too.

But how and when do we think this will actually happen? Frank Chen (head of research, deal, and investing at Andreessen Horowitz), considers these questions and use cases in this set of videos, which can be viewed together all at once or separately...
