## Description

What kind of company do you want to build? Are you spending enough time creating company culture versus finding product/market fit? Are you mindful about the example you set as a startup CEO or founder?

Serial entrepreneur Justin Kan (@justinkan) shares his experience on how to mindfully create company culture, design a core of shared values, and enable your people to make the best decisions as a team. 

Justin has founded several companies, including Twitch (sold to Amazon) and now Atrium, a software-powered law firm.

This clip is part of a16z Entrepreneurship Advice, a series of mini-lessons on how to start, build, and scale startups.
