## Description

Autonomy is no longer a moonshot—it’s a market. And increasingly, it’s a battlefield.

Recorded live at the 2025 American Dynamism Summit, this discussion features a16z General Partner Erin Price-Wright sitting down with Qasar Younis (cofounder & CEO, Applied Intuition) and Jason Brown (Head of Government Business, Applied Intuition) to explore how vehicle intelligence and autonomy are reshaping defense, industry, and the future of transportation. They cover:

- Why the next decade is about deploying autonomy, not debating it
- The convergence of commercial and military autonomy stacks
- How to retrofit 100-year-old defense platforms with cutting-edge AI
- Why dual-use companies can—and must—set the benchmark
- What it takes to win the software-defined arms race


From self-driving ride-hail in San Francisco to autonomous naval vessels, the future isn’t just autonomous—it’s already in production.
