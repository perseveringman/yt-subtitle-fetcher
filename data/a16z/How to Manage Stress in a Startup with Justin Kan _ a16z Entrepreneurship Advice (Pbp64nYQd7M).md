## Description

Creating and leading a startup can be highly stressful. Expectations for repeat founders increase with each successive startup. How does a startup CEO manage that stress?

Serial entrepreneur Justin Kan (@justinkan) shares his techniques on how to manage stress in a startup, based on what he’s learned during his journey of founding multiple startups, including Twitch (sold to Amazon) and now Atrium, a software-powered law firm.

This clip is part of a16z Entrepreneurship Advice, a series of mini-lessons on how to start, build, and scale startups.
