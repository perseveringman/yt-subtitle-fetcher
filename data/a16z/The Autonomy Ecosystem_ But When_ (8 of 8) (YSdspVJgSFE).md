## Description

Why should we be stripped from the joy of owning and/or driving a car? These are just some of the objections to the vision of an autonomous, self-driving future. But let’s not overly romanticize driving! The realities are far more often much more stressful, from time to safety. The bigger question is: When will all of this actually happen? 5 years, 50 years, 100 years? 

Frank Chen (head of research, deal, and investing at Andreessen Horowitz), considers these questions in this set of videos -- which can be viewed together all at once or separately -- that goes beyond just self-driving cars to the overall autonomy ecosystem...
